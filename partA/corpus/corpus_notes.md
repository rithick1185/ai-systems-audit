# A1. Multilingual Evaluation Corpus

## Corpus languages

The evaluation corpus contains four languages:

- English
- Hindi
- Kannada
- Tamil

English is used as the baseline language. Hindi, Kannada, and Tamil are included to evaluate multilingual and Dravidian-language tokenization behavior.

## Corpus domain

The corpus contains short, general-domain sentences covering:

- Agriculture
- Education
- Technology
- Daily communication
- Public information

The sentences are designed to represent normal user prompts rather than isolated words.

The initial corpus contains at least 20 sentences per language:

4 languages × 20 sentences
